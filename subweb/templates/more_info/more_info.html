More Information

Vision

To transform Rajasthan into a lush green state by mobilizing collective efforts for large-scale tree plantation, ensuring a sustainable environment for future generations.

Mission

To plant 7 crore trees across All districts of Rajasthan, leveraging community participation and advanced technology for effective monitoring and management of the plantation process.

About

Mukhyamantri Vriksharopan Maha Abhiyan is an ambitious initiative by the Government of Rajasthan aimed at significantly increasing the state's green cover. This large-scale plantation drive involves both government departments and citizens, fostering a community-driven approach to environmental conservation. The success of this initiative will be monitored using the Hariyalo-Rajasthan mobile application, which allows users to register, track, and ensure the growth and survival of planted trees.

Benefits

  • Conservation of biodiversity
  • Soil and water conservation
  • Mitigation of climate change
  • Community participation
  • Promotion of urban greenery
