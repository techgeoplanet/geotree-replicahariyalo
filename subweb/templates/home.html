"Haryalo-Rajasthan" (Ek Ped Maa ke Naam)

With the vision to develop Rajasthan as a green state, the FY 2024-25 budget has set a goal through the Mukhyamantri Vriksharopan Maha Abhiyan to involve every family in planting and nurturing 7 crore trees. To sustain this effort, the mission "Haryalo-Rajasthan" has been declared as a Multi-Sectoral Programme.
